# Experimental Anchor Candidates for Surrogate → CALPHAD → Experiment Validation

Scope: Fe-Cr-Ni(−C), Cr 0–35 at%, Ni 0–30 at%, C 0–5 at% (≈ ≤1.1 wt% C), Fe balance, T = 700–2000 K.
Categories: **A** = isopleths/vertical sections with experimental phase-boundary points · **B** = measured invariant (eutectic/peritectic) temperatures · **C** = measured phase fractions (δ-ferrite/σ/austenite) after anneals.
Verification status (all checked 2026-09-29): link resolves + content confirmed = **verified**; landing page found but PDF behind paywall = **paywalled (abstract verified)**; found only via bibliographic index = **index record only**.

wt% → at% quick reference: 18 wt% Cr ≈ 18.7 at%; 8 wt% Ni ≈ 7.4 at%; 1.1 wt% C ≈ 5 at%.

## TOP 3 recommended anchors

### 1. (A+B) Yamada, Umeda, Suzuki, Arakine, Kihara & Kimura 1987 — open access ★ primary anchor
**"Fe-Cr-Ni系鉄高濃度領域における液相面及び固相面の測定" (Liquidus and solidus surfaces of the Fe-rich Fe-Cr-Ni system)**, *Tetsu-to-Hagané* (Iron and Steel Journal of Japan) **73**(14): 1676–1683. DOI: [10.2355/tetsutohagane1955.73.14_1676](https://doi.org/10.2355/tetsutohagane1955.73.14_1676). PDF: <https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tetsutohagane1955/73/14/73_14_1676/_pdf> — **verified open access** (J-STAGE, CC BY-NC-ND, English abstract).
- **Measured**: liquidus + solidus surfaces of the Fe-rich corner; L/δ and L/γ tie-lines (stationary-melting method); DTA liquidus temperatures; α- vs γ-primary phase regions; the **peritectic–eutectic line** (found shifted toward Fe-Cr side between 10–15 wt% Cr) with the **peritectic–eutectic transition point ≈ 15 wt% Cr–10 wt% Ni**; comparison with Schürmann et al.
- **Composition/T window**: covers the 5–25 wt% Cr / 0–20 wt% Ni Fe-rich region (in range) at solidification temperatures (~1700–1810 K, within 2000 K cap).
- **Digitizability**: plots (Figs. 6–10, incl. an isopleth-type liquidus/solidus vs composition figure) → digitization required, but high-contrast scans and numerical axes; English abstract states the key numbers. **Rank: plot (OK).**
- **Verdict**: the single best open source combining an isopleth-like section, boundary lines, and an invariant (peritectic–eutectic) feature in exactly our composition space.

### 2. (B) Drozdová, Smetana et al. 2017 — open access, tabulated ★ invariant-T anchor
**"Temperatures of Liquidus, Solidus and Peritectic Transformation of Fe-C-Cr Based Alloys"**, METAL 2017 conference (Brno, CZ, 24–26 May 2017), pp. 65–70. PDF (verified, downloaded & tables read): <https://www.confer.cz/metal/2017/download/1347-phase-diagram-of-the-fe-c-cr-ternary-system.pdf>
- **Measured** (Table 2, °C, DTA / direct-TA-heating / direct-TA-cooling): alloy A (0.308 C–1.058 Cr wt%): T_S 1447/1449/1451, **T_P 1486/1484/–**, T_L 1498/1503/1499; alloy B (0.320 C–1.540 Cr): T_S 1445/1447/1437, **T_P 1471/1473/–**, T_L 1498/1501/1495; alloy C (0.380 C–4.990 Cr): T_S 1397/1405/1410, **T_P 1438/1441/–**, T_L 1474/1480/1476. Calculated Thermo-Calc(TCFE8)/IDS values included side-by-side.
- **Range fit**: Cr ≤ 5 wt% ✓, C ≈ 1.4–1.6 at% ✓, Ni = 0 (boundary of domain) ✓, T = 1670–1774 K ✓. **No Mo/Mn/N confounders.**
- **Digitizability**: **table in text → best rank.**
- **Verdict**: cleanest measured peritectic/solidus/liquidus triple-point data in our system; ideal B anchor. Caveat: probes only the low-Cr, Ni-free face of the domain.

### 3. (C) Forgas Júnior, Otubo & Magnabosco 2016 — open access, tabulated ★ phase-fraction anchor
**"Ferrite Quantification Methodologies for Duplex Stainless Steel"**, *Journal of Aerospace Technology and Management* **8**(3): 357–362. DOI: [10.5028/jatm.v8i3.653](https://doi.org/10.5028/jatm.v8i3.653). PDF verified (downloaded & tables read): <https://jatm.com.br/jatm/article/download/653/536/3967>
- **Measured**: UNS S31803 (22.07 Cr–5.68 Ni–3.20 Mo–0.17 N–0.017 C wt%) solution-treated 30 min at 1000/1100/1200 °C, water quenched. **Table 3 ferrite vol%**: quantitative metallography **41.5 ± 0.9 / 48.1 ± 0.8 / 58.5 ± 1.5**; ferritoscope 39.8/46.2/57.0; XRD 58.4/68.5/87.2 (texture-biased); Thermo-Calc(TCFE7) equilibrium 40.4/51.3/66.4.
- **Range fit**: Cr 22.07 wt% ≈ 23.4 at% ✓, Ni 5.68 wt% ≈ 5.3 at% ✓, C ✓, T = 1273–1473 K ✓. **Caveats: 3.20 wt% Mo and 0.17 wt% N are outside the Fe-Cr-Ni-C model** (treat as fixed nuisance composition or flag exclusion); 30-min holds may be short of equilibrium; prefer the metallography column (paper itself says it is the most assertive method).
- **Digitizability**: **table in text → best rank.**
- **Verdict**: only open, tabulated δ-ferrite-vs-temperature dataset found inside our T/composition window; use with the above caveats stated in the paper.

## Recommended validation plan

**Use anchors 1–3 (one per category) as the primary validation set.** (a) *Boundary/isopleth (A)*: digitize Yamada et al. (1987) liquidus/solidus isopleth points and the peritectic–eutectic line; run the surrogate→full-CALPHAD chain at those compositions and report **ΔT_liquidus/solidus in K (RMSE + max |ΔT|)** and the composition deviation of the predicted P–E line in wt% Cr/Ni (experiment–experiment scatter in the source itself is a few K / ~1 wt%, which sets the noise floor). (b) *Invariant temperatures (B)*: compare predicted T_S/T_P/T_L for Drozdová et al. (2017) alloys A–C against the DTA and TA-heating columns of their Table 2; report per-alloy **ΔT in K** and mean absolute ΔT (their own Thermo-Calc-vs-experiment deviations of ~2–20 K give a realistic acceptance band; target |ΔT| ≤ 15 K for T_L/T_S, ≤ 25 K for T_P). (c) *Phase fractions (C)*: predict equilibrium δ-ferrite fraction for S31803 at 1000/1100/1200 °C and compare to the **quantitative-metallography column** of Forgas Júnior et al. (2016) Table 3; report **Δf_δ in vol%** (target ≤ 5 vol%; the metallography–ferritoscope spread of ~1–2 vol% and the paper's own TC-vs-measured gaps of 1–8 vol% define the noise floor), excluding or explicitly fixing the Mo/N balance. State all three experimental protocols (30 min holds, DTA at 5–10 K/min, water quench) and the caveat that short holds under-approach equilibrium.

**Fallbacks if a source is not digitizable or not accessible**: (1) for A, swap Yamada → Nishino & Kagawa (1972) Fig. 16 (open, but only at 25Cr–20Ni and C ≤ 1.1 wt%); (2) for B, swap to the T_L table of Smetana et al. (METAL 1347/1343, both open and tabulated); (3) for C, if a tabulated open δ-ferrite dataset is rejected for its Mo confounder, fall back to Arh et al. (2021) δ-ferrite-vs-anneal table (open; same Mo caveat) or, with library access, Mundt & Hoffmeister (1983) — the cleanest equilibrium ternary δ-fraction set; (4) if no experimental anchor in a category survives, use a **calculated-but-published isopleth** (Qiu 1993; Hillert & Qiu 1992) and label the result explicitly as *consistency with published CALPHAD*, not experimental validation — it checks the surrogate→CALPHAD leg only and carries no experimental error bar.
