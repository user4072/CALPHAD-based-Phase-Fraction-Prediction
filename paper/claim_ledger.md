## Verification policy (used by both the ledger and the audit)

A written value **passes** when

```
|written − recomputed| ≤ max(0.5 × ULP(written), 1.5 % × |recomputed|)
```

where ULP is the unit of the last printed digit (`0.5` for integers, `0.05` for
one decimal, `0.005` for two decimals, …). Counts must match **exactly**.
Inequalities (`≤ 5×10⁻⁸`) pass with 1 % relative slack. Range claims
(`a--b`) must have *both* endpoints pass against the min/max of a documented
population; when a claim admits more than one reading of the population, the
reading used is named in the row and any alternative reading is recorded.

Artifact roots: `models/` (M), `analysis_revision/` (A), `data/raw/` (D),
`paper/anchor_data/` (AN), `paper/tab/` (T, generated by `tables.py`).
